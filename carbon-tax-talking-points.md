# CFIB Carbon Tax Victory - Sales Talking Points

## 🎯 **The Elevator Pitch**
*"CFIB just secured $2.5 billion in carbon tax rebates for small businesses after a 5-year fight with Ottawa. This wasn't automatic - it took 27,000 business owners signing petitions and relentless advocacy. Without CFIB's lobbying power, this money would have stayed with the government. That's what 100,000 voices can do."*

---

## 💰 **Key Numbers to Memorize**

- **$2.5 billion** - Total returned to small businesses (2019-2024)
- **$623 million** - Final payment distributed December 2024
- **600,000 businesses** - Number of businesses receiving rebates
- **5 years** - How long CFIB fought to get this done
- **27,000 signatures** - Petitions delivered to government
- **40%** - Portion of carbon tax paid by small businesses
- **5%** - What they get back in rebates (was 9%, cut to 5%)
- **100,000 members** - CFIB's collective voice

---

## 🏆 **Timeline of CFIB's Win**

### 2019
- Federal carbon tax introduced
- Small businesses pay 40% of the tax
- Rebate system promised but not delivered

### 2020-2023
- CFIB launches aggressive advocacy campaign
- Delivers 27,000+ signed petitions to Parliament
- Coordinates with provincial governments
- Relentless media pressure

### November 2024
- Government announces final $623M payment
- CFIB secures tax-free status for rebates
- Extends eligibility deadline

### June 2025
- Government confirms rebates will be tax-free
- CFIB prevents CRA from taxing the rebate as income

### December 2025
- Final rebates distributed to 600,000 businesses

## 🎤 **Talking Points by Scenario**

### **When Prospect Says: "What has CFIB actually done for me?"**

*"Let me show you something concrete. [Pull up CFIB page] CFIB just returned $2.5 billion in carbon tax rebates to businesses like yours. For a 5-employee business in Ontario, that's about $2,000 over 5 years. But here's the important part - this didn't happen automatically. The government had this money for 5 years and wasn't giving it back. CFIB had to fight - 27,000 petition signatures, coordination with provinces, constant pressure on Ottawa. Without that advocacy, you wouldn't have seen a dime."*

### **When Prospect Says: "I can handle government stuff myself"**

*"Sure, you could try. But let me ask - did you know the government was sitting on $2.5 billion in carbon tax rebates for 5 years? Did you know they were planning to tax the rebate as income until CFIB stopped them? That's the difference between going solo and having 100,000 business owners fighting together. CFIB has direct lines to ministers, research teams analyzing every policy, and the leverage to actually move the needle."*

### **When Prospect Says: "Membership is too expensive"**

*"Let's talk ROI. The carbon tax rebate alone paid for 3-5 years of CFIB membership for most businesses. And that's just one win. Add the WSIB surplus rebate ($250/employee in 2026), payment processing savings, HR document library, and you're looking at 10x-20x return on a $588 membership. The question isn't whether you can afford to join - it's whether you can afford NOT to."*

### **When Prospect Says: "I'm not incorporated so I don't qualify for carbon rebate"**

*"You're right that the carbon rebate only went to CCPCs. But here's what CFIB is doing about that - they're actively lobbying to expand eligibility. More importantly, this carbon tax fight shows you HOW they operate: identify an unfair policy, mobilize members, apply pressure, get results. Right now they're fighting on 15+ other issues that affect ALL businesses - incorporated or not. Plus, they can help you evaluate whether incorporating makes sense for your business."*

## ⚠️ **What NOT to Say**

❌ "The carbon tax is over, so this doesn't matter anymore"
- CFIB is still fighting for fairness on past taxes and future policies

❌ "Everyone got the rebate automatically"
- No - it took 5 years of CFIB pressure to make this happen

❌ "This is just one small win"
- $2.5 billion is massive, plus it demonstrates CFIB's advocacy effectiveness

❌ "You would have gotten this anyway"
- Government sat on the money for 5 years with no plan to return it
