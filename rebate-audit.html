Rebate Eligibility Audit

Diagnose why they didn't get their money - and how to fix it

🇨🇦 Carbon Rebate Eligibility (Small Business)

1. Is the business incorporated as a CCPC (Canadian Controlled Private Corporation)?
2. Does the business have 1-499 employees with T4s issued?
3. Was the 2023 T4 Summary filed by July 15, 2024?
4. Is the business located in Ontario (or another fuel-charge province)?

🏥 WSIB Surplus Distribution Eligibility

1. Does the business have an active WSIB account?
2. Is the business up to date with WSIB premium payments?
3. Is the business located in Ontario?
4. Is the business in good standing with no major safety violations?
